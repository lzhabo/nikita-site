About the guide

I'm Nikita — a native English speaker and a Political Economy and History student at King's College London. I've lived in England for six years, and my obsession is a single question: how do politics and money physically build cities?

London is the best city in the world for that question. Every alley is someone's deal. Every building is someone's victory or someone's loss. I've been here long enough to learn how to read it — and not so long that it stopped feeling extraordinary.

My tours aren't about dates and facts. They're about making history feel alive — so that after our walk, you start seeing the city differently. Guests say the hours disappear. I think that's the point.
